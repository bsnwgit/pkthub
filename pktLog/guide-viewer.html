Viewer Overview

The Viewer role provides read-only access to the pktLog dashboard, log browser, and alert events. Viewers can observe what is happening on the network's logging infrastructure but cannot modify data, export results, or change any configuration.

If you need to export data or investigate an incident in depth, ask your administrator to upgrade your account to the Analyst role.

Logging In

Navigate to the pktLog URL provided by your administrator. You will be prompted for a username and password. Enter the credentials your administrator provided. Your session will remain active for the duration configured by your admin (typically 8 hours).

If you cannot log in or your account is not active, contact your pktLog administrator.

Dashboard

After logging in you will land on the Dashboard — a real-time overview of all syslog activity across the platform.

Browsing Logs

Navigate to Logs in the top menu to browse the syslog event stream.

Browsing the Log Table

The Logs page shows a paginated table of syslog events, most recent first. You can scroll through pages to see older events. Click any row to expand it and see the full event details including the original raw syslog message.

As a Viewer, you can use the time range selector and basic filters (host, severity) to narrow what you see, but you cannot export or save search results.

Viewers cannot export log data. To download results for reporting or investigation, request that an Analyst perform the export.

Viewing Alerts

Navigate to Alerts to see alert events generated by the platform's alert rules.

Reading the Alerts Page

The Alerts page shows all fired alert events with their status (Open, Acknowledged, Resolved) and the rule that triggered them. As a Viewer you can read alerts to understand what conditions have been detected, but you cannot acknowledge or resolve them — those actions are reserved for Analysts and Admins.

If you see an alert you believe requires immediate attention, notify an Analyst or Administrator directly.
